Recommended: continuous scroll

Keep the exercise in one document, with source-specific section jumps and a collapsible full evidence gallery. Silhouette Comparison follows YESes, MAYBES/NOs, LOOK FOR and RECORD; other exercises keep their own structure. This makes earlier context visible and removes required stage-switching clicks. The separate-stage alternative remains available in the exercise so you can compare reading comfort using exactly the same answers.
