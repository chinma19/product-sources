# Evidence purpose and source-stage audit

Draft source mapping for the isolated editorial prototype. **Only step 12 is actively rendered.** This file does not approve a redesign of all 68 steps, change any response or add any exercise.

Primary source: approved `content/workbook.json` (Draft 3 with approved R1–R10 overlays). Page numbers refer to its original manuscript inventory. Supporting references: Product Build Specification v1.0 §§07–15, §§39/49/53 and approved R3/R4/R6/R9/R10.

## How to read the collection mapping

- `collections` lists eligible existing evidence, not an instruction to show every listed gallery. Reasons distinguish direct observation, supporting references, preparation, and saved-answer-only work.
- Never add both collections merely because an exercise belongs to Personality or Synthesis. The real collections are **Recent Outfits** (`recent`) and **Inspiration** (`inspiration`); “Real Life” is reflection, not a third photo library.
- Keep collections distinct when a comparison calls for both. Page 23 uses Inspiration first, and may bring in Recent Outfits for its explicit actual-clothes comparison. Page 42 is conditional own-versus-saved context. Page 52 explicitly chooses **one outfit from either source**; an array with both values must not become two automatically opened full galleries.
- Body supporting references may reuse the Recent Outfits established on p8; illustrations, saved selections, comfort experiences and personal boundaries remain the exercise evidence where stated. A photo cannot establish what feels comfortable.
- Part 5 primarily reuses completed records. A link to a source answer can reopen relevant evidence without automatically displaying both whole collections. Page 62 may optionally revisit Recent Outfits because p31 explicitly used them.
- Cues below and in JSON are literal text from the approved content. Where the cue is inherited context (p8 for silhouette observation, p31 for signature carry-forward), the reason identifies that context.

## Source stages are not universally four steps

Page 4 teaches the overall **LOOK → CHOOSE → REFLECT → RECORD** method. **Only exercise page 8 contains all four standalone headings verbatim.** Page 11 contains LOOK and CHOOSE; p13 combines LOOK + CHOOSE; p15 uses LOOK and SORT; p25 uses PLACE YOURSELF; p37 is a 100-point time map; p47 has three Pattern/Evidence/Meaning records; p63 has three outfit-direction structures. These are legitimate different source structures.

### Represented step 12: preserve this source order

| Source block | Exact source cue / heading | Existing controls |
|---|---|---|
| YES selections | “Look at the silhouettes you marked YES.” / “Which descriptions appear most often?” | `p12.choices1` |
| MAYBES + NOs | “Now look at your MAYBES.” / “What makes you hesitate?” / “And your NOs.” / “What do they have in common?” | `silhouette.notes.1`, `silhouette.notes.2` |
| LOOK FOR | “LOOK FOR” / “Do you repeatedly prefer:” | `p12.choices2` |
| RECORD | “RECORD” / the three original record prompts | `silhouette.notes.3`, `.4`, `.5` |

The prototype’s earlier global-four-stage grouping was a navigation adaptation, not four literal p12 source headings. It put `p12.choices2` before the MAYBES/NOs prompts and placed `.5` in REFLECT. For source-order fidelity, use the blocks above; do not label invented stages as original manuscript headings.

Each future mapped exercise needs its own audited source blocks and collection-selection mode. Preserve original labels, options, questions, examples and order. Continuous flow renders those blocks in order; an optional separate-view comparison uses the same variable-length blocks. No fixed four-slot schema, forced completion or automatic interpretation follows from the general method. For unrepresented pages, show original guidance as one read-only block until a specific plan is approved.
